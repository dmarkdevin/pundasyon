# Code of conduct

Pundasyon follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the people using and building this project.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact options, or by opening a private security advisory if no other channel is available. Every report will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban anyone from the project for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
